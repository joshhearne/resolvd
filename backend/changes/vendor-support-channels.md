**Vendor support channels:** set up how to open a ticket with each vendor's own helpdesk under **Admin → Companies → (vendor) → Support**: their support email (role addresses like support@ are fine here), portal link, and phone line with call-tree and account notes, each recommended for a priority range (say phone for P1–P2, email or portal for P3–P5). Tickets in the vendor's project get a **Vendor support** tab for agents with the right options first: forward the ticket to their support email, open their portal, or call. Matching new tickets also get an agent-only note with the recommended channels, and another when a priority change calls for a different one. A pattern on the title / description or a form field value keeps the note to the tickets that are really about that vendor, and vendors can be allowed into extra projects (for example an MSP's customer projects).
