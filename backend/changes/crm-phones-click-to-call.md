- **Company people:** edit a member's or contact's details under **Admin → Companies**, including job title, location, extension, direct line (DID) and mobile.
- **Click to call:** a ticket's new **Contacts** tab lists the requester and attached contacts with tap-to-call buttons for their main line + extension, direct line and mobile. On a phone they dial directly; on a desktop they open your calling app.
