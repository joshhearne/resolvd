**Calling apps:** admins choose which calling apps the ticket **Contacts** tab's call buttons can open (phone app, Webex, Microsoft Teams, Zoom Phone, RingCentral, Dialpad, or your own) under **Admin → Calling apps**, and everyone picks theirs under **Account → Preferences → Call with**. For apps like Webex, internal colleagues can be called on their extension alone, and every button keeps a **Phone app** fallback.
